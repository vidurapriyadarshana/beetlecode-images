Internship Period

The Company will engage you as an intern from 07 September 2026 to 06 March 2027, a period of six (6) months.

During the period of your internship, your attitude to training, efficiency, conduct, attendance, punctuality, discipline, and related matters will be monitored and evaluated. During your internship period, the Company, without any notice or reasons, may determine termination of your internship in case your performance as an intern is not up to the expectations of the management.

Working Hours

Your working hours will be from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., completing nine (9) hours of work per day, on a hybrid basis (a combination of on-site and remote work as determined by the Company).

During the period of your internship, it is a compulsory requirement that you attend all training classes and programs provided by the Company, if any, on weekdays, weekends, and holidays, as scheduled.

Reporting

You will report on all matters pertaining to your functions to the Director or to any other person as directed by the Director.

Secrecy of Information

You shall take all precautions necessary to keep the secrecy of all records or information which relate to the business in which the Company or its associates are engaged, and which will come into your purview during your internship.

You will not discuss with any person, company, or organization whatsoever any matter relating to the business of the Company, or any trade secrets which may come to your possession or knowledge, so as to prevent such records, knowledge, or information from passing into the possession of any person, company, or organization whatsoever. Breach of this will result in instant discontinuation of the internship agreement.

While expressing our pleasure in offering you an internship at our Company, we expect you to contribute towards the achievement of the goals and objectives of our organization.
